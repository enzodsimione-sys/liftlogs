# Lift Log 1.1: things to check on your phone (about 25 minutes)

These are the things the computer tests cannot prove, because there was no phone to try them on. Tick them off in order. If anything behaves differently, write down the step number and what you saw.

## A. Before you start
1. **In the old version (1.0), save a backup first.** Settings tab › **Save backup to a file** › pick Google Drive or a folder. The message must say "Backup saved and checked".
2. Install version 1.1 **over** 1.0 (download the new file and install it; do **not** uninstall). Open the app: your old workouts must still be in the History tab, your routines and settings unchanged.

## B. The notification and the alert (the most important check)
3. Tap **Start Day A**. If Android asks about notifications, tap **Allow**.
4. Log one set. The rest timer starts. **Swipe down from the top of the screen without opening the app.** You should see a "Lift Log" notification: "Resting" with the routine name, a countdown to the end of the rest, the next exercise and set with the weight and reps ("Next: ...") and a **Skip rest** button. (Between rests, the countdown is replaced by a clock counting how long the workout has gone.)
5. Open another app (music, a browser). Pull the shade down again: the countdown keeps running. Tap **Skip rest**: the countdown goes away and the notification shows what is next.
6. Log another set and **lock the phone**. When the rest ends the phone must **vibrate**, and the notification turns into "Rest over". On the lock screen it may only say "Workout in progress" (that is on purpose: your numbers stay private).
7. If the buzz is missing or late: phone Settings › Battery › Background usage limits › make sure Lift Log is **not** under Sleeping apps (add it to **Never sleeping apps**). A late alarm can briefly show a negative countdown; that is cosmetic.
8. Tap the notification: the app opens on the workout. Do this once with the app closed (swiped out of Recents) too.
9. Settings tab › **WORKOUT NOTIFICATION** (its own section): switch it Off: the notification disappears. Switch it On again: it comes back. If Android lets you swipe the notification away, it should stay away for that workout and come back for the next one.
10. If Settings says "Notifications are switched off", tap **Open notification settings** and check the button opens the right screen.

## C. Killing the app
11. Mid-workout, type a weight but do **not** log it. Open Recents and swipe Lift Log away. Open it again: you must be back in the same workout with the typed weight.

## D. Backups
12. Settings tab › **AUTOMATIC COPIES** › **Choose a folder**. Android will not let you pick the top of storage or Downloads: make a new folder (for example "Lift Log backups") and pick that. Finish a workout, then open that folder in the Files app: a file called `liftlog-auto-` plus today's date must be there. Settings must show "Last automatic copy ... saved and checked".
13. Finish a second workout the same day: still only one file for today. ("Days of copies to keep" means that many different days that have a copy; older ones are removed and nothing else in the folder is ever touched.)
14. A folder on the phone is lost with the phone, so the 14-day reminder still asks for a copy on Google Drive. That is intended.
15. Settings tab › **Import a backup file**: pick the backup from step 1. The app shows what it would replace and keeps a safety copy. You can cancel here.

## E. New in 1.1 (try each once)
16. **History › Log a past workout**: pick Day A, pick yesterday on the calendar, Create. Add a set to an exercise (its numbers are pre-filled from last time) and edit one. Leave and come back: it stays. Create another one and leave without adding a set: it disappears.
17. **History › open a workout you finished less than 3 hours ago › Reopen workout**: you land in the live workout and can log another set; finish it again. An older workout offers no Reopen (it can still be edited).
18. **Routine editor (Workout tab › Edit)**: put a thumb on the three bars at the right of an exercise and slide it up or down; release over another exercise. A superset pair moves together. The arrows still work.
19. **Add exercise (during a workout) › + New exercise**: create "Test lift" and Save: it joins the workout straight away (no extra tap). Check the workout you were in has not lost anything. (If you open it from **Swap**, it asks the usual "just today or the routine too?" question.)
20. **+ Add set** on an exercise: a strip asks "Make it 4 sets in Day A?" (once per exercise) with **Not now** / **Make it 4 sets**. Try both buttons (on different exercises).
21. Look at a superset: both exercises are in one card; tap the other exercise's heading and it opens.
22. In the page of a finished workout, exercises you skipped are one line ("Not done: ..." with Show).
23. While a rest is running, log a set: "Undo" appears next to "Note" for five seconds and the rest bar says which set was logged.

## F. Looks
24. Open every tab and a few screens and look at the **bottom edge** (gesture navigation and, if you try it, 3-button navigation): nothing important may be hidden behind the phone's bars. The same at the top while a rest is running.
25. Settings › About shows the start-up time in milliseconds the app measured. Tell me the number if it is over 1500.
26. Dark and light themes: Settings › Appearance.

If everything above works you have verified what I could not. Thank you.
